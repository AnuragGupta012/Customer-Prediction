# AI-Powered Customer Churn Prediction & Revenue Recovery Dashboard

## Overview

The AI-Powered Customer Churn Prediction & Revenue Recovery Dashboard is a machine-learning-based application designed to identify customers who are at risk of churn.

Customer churn can directly impact business revenue. By predicting customers who are more likely to leave, businesses can prioritize retention efforts, understand customer behaviour, and take proactive actions to reduce potential revenue loss.

The project combines data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, machine learning, interactive visualization, and business-focused insights in a Streamlit dashboard.

## Business Problem

Customer churn is a major challenge for businesses because losing existing customers can result in recurring revenue loss.

Traditional analysis often identifies churn only after a customer has already left.

This project aims to provide an early-warning mechanism by predicting customers who may be at risk of churn.

## Objectives

* Analyze customer behaviour and churn patterns.
* Clean and preprocess customer data.
* Identify important factors associated with customer churn.
* Train a machine learning model for churn prediction.
* Provide interactive KPI dashboards and visualizations.
* Identify customers who may require retention attention.
* Help businesses prioritize actions that can reduce potential revenue loss.

## Key Features

### 1. Customer Data Analysis

The application analyzes customer information and prepares the data for machine learning.

### 2. Churn Prediction

A machine learning workflow is used to predict whether a customer is likely to churn.

### 3. KPI Dashboard

The dashboard provides business-oriented metrics and visualizations to understand customer behaviour and churn performance.

### 4. Interactive Visualizations

Plotly is used to create interactive charts that make customer patterns easier to understand.

### 5. Customer Risk Identification

The prediction workflow helps identify customers who may be at higher risk of leaving.

### 6. Revenue Recovery Perspective

The predicted high-risk customers can be prioritized for retention campaigns, helping businesses attempt to prevent future revenue loss.

## Business Impact

The project changes churn analysis from a reactive process into a more proactive approach.

Instead of waiting for customers to leave, businesses can use churn predictions to:

* Identify potentially at-risk customers.
* Prioritize high-risk customer segments.
* Design targeted retention campaigns.
* Monitor churn-related KPIs.
* Reduce potential revenue leakage.
* Support data-driven customer retention decisions.
